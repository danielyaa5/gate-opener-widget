Gate Opener

Your gate, at a glance.

Control your Tuya gate from Mac or iPhone. Optional Ring camera checks show Open, Closed or Unclear, the latest picture and a link to Live View.

Free and open source (MIT). macOS 14 or later, signed and notarized. The iPhone app is built from source.

The demo shows the latest source build. Camera pictures, camera-based actions and automatic closing are available from source; the signed Mac download is the earlier 1.0.0 release.

How it works

Bridge on your Mac

A small Python service on an always-on Mac talks to the gate controller over your LAN with its Tuya local key.

Signed requests

Every request is signed with your own secret, timestamped and single-use, and reaches the bridge over HTTPS, for example with Tailscale Funnel.

One pulse, like a remote

Open / Close sends one momentary pulse. Your controller decides whether that opens, closes or stops the gate.

In the latest source build

See the latest picture

The apps show the camera image and its original capture time. Older pictures are labeled; an unclear view stays unclear.

Open Ring Live View

Jump from the app to the configured camera in Ring. Camera credentials stay on your bridge Mac.

Optional closing rules

Use an overnight timeout or fresh TagTracker distance evidence. A close requires a fresh, confirmed Open observation; the distance rule attempts only once until a confirmed return Home.

The Mac app also quits when its last window closes, removing its Dock icon. The desktop widget and bridge services continue independently.

Get started

  1. Set up the bridge on an always-on Mac on the gate's network. The README walks through it.
  2. On that Mac, run .venv/bin/python scripts/setup_code.py --url https://your-mac.your-tailnet.ts.net and copy the code.
  3. Open Gate Opener, click Paste and the app connects. Add the widget from the desktop's Edit Widgets.

Works with Tuya-based momentary gate controllers that use data point 1. An accepted pulse is not proof of movement. Optional camera observations can be unclear or delayed; keep the gate's own safety sensors working.
